📖 What is the Wheel Strategy?

The Wheel is an options strategy that generates income by selling cash-secured puts, and if assigned, selling covered calls.

Phase 1 (Put Selling): Sell OTM puts to collect premium. If stock stays above strike, keep premium. If assigned, you own the stock at a discount.

Phase 2 (Call Selling): If assigned, sell covered calls above your cost basis. If called away, you profit from premium + capital gains.

Key Benefits: Consistent income, lower risk than buying stock outright, works in sideways or slightly bullish markets.

✅ Why the Wheel Works

1. Time Decay: Options lose value as expiration approaches, benefiting the seller

2. Volatility Premium: High IV stocks pay more premium (but carry more risk)

3. Downside Protection: OTM strikes provide cushion against drops

4. Consistent Income: Regular premium collection vs. waiting for stock appreciation
